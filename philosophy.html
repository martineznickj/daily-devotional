Philosophy & Theology

What we believe, and the scripture behind it

Every idea on this page is tested the same way. A claim, then the text. Each carries the move that got us there.

The One Phrase

God makes his home with us. We break it. He pays to get it back.

Genesis 1:1In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth.

Revelation 21:3Behold, the dwelling place of God is with man. He will dwell with them, and they will be his people.

Jeremiah 31:33I will be their God, and they shall be my people.

The common theme is redemption. The spine is covenant, six of them: with Adam, Noah, Abraham, Moses, David, and the new covenant in Christ. Every book of the Bible is a covenant made, broken, remembered, or fulfilled.

The Core Philosophy

Every worldview answers five questions. Here is how the text answers them.

1. What is real?

God. Not one being among many, but being itself, the ground of everything. Reality is made, intentional, and good.

Genesis 1:1In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth.

2. What is wrong?

The fall. The human condition is a broken relationship, a will turned inward, not bad luck or ignorance.

Romans 3:23All have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.

3. What is the fix?

Grace, not merit. This is the hinge, and the sharpest difference from every merit based system.

Ephesians 2:8-9By grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God, not a result of works.

4. How do I live?

Love. Not rules. A person, then people.

Matthew 22:37-39You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind. You shall love your neighbor as yourself.

Galatians 5:6Faith working through love.

5. Where is it going?

New creation. Restoration of everything, body included.

2 Corinthians 5:17If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away.

The Deep Doctrines

The Trinity

One God, three persons. The Father above, the Son with us, the Spirit within. The word never appears in the Bible. It is a summary of three facts the text asserts at once.

Deuteronomy 6:4Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one.

John 1:1In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.

Matthew 28:19Baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit.

The return at the end of the ages

The bookend completed. He comes back the way he left. Restoration, not escape. Nobody knows when, and the text says stop guessing.

Acts 1:11This Jesus, who was taken up from you into heaven, will come in the same way.

Revelation 21:4He will wipe away every tear from their eyes, and death shall be no more.

Matthew 24:36Concerning that day and hour no one knows, but the Father only.

Grace and predestination

The deepest tension in the book. The text says both, in the same letters. They are two views of the same door: from your side you push it open, from God's side it was already held for you.

Ephesians 1:4-5He chose us in him before the foundation of the world. In love he predestined us for adoption.

John 3:16Whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life.

2 Peter 3:9Not wishing that any should perish, but that all should reach repentance.

The Hidden Structure

Beneath the surface the book is engineered. These are the load bearing patterns.

The bookend

It opens with God creating. It closes with God dwelling. One arc from God with us to God with us.

Genesis 1:1 · Revelation 21:3In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth. Behold, the dwelling place of God is with man.

Typology

The Old Testament is a shadow. Earlier figures rehearse Christ before he arrives: Adam, the Passover lamb, the serpent lifted up, Jonah's three days.

Galatians 3:24The law was our guardian until Christ came.

The exchange

The whole machine runs on substitution. The innocent bears the guilty. That is why every covenant carries a sacrifice.

1 Peter 3:18Christ also suffered once for sins, the righteous for the unrighteous, that he might bring us to God.

The mystery

The hidden thing is not a code to crack. It is that God's plan was to dwell inside the person, not just near them.

Colossians 1:27The mystery hidden for ages, now revealed: Christ in you, the hope of glory.

The One Line

It's a love story where God won't stop coming back.
